01 / PROFILE

About me

An academic profile shaped by electrical engineering, photovoltaic modeling and student leadership.

I am a final-year undergraduate student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Jamalpur Science & Technology University, Bangladesh. I am currently in 4th Year, 1st Semester, with classes ongoing.

My academic interests include solar photovoltaics, renewable energy systems, semiconductor devices and computational methods for photovoltaic-device analysis. My SCAPS-1D work examines lead-free double-absorber solar-cell structures and the influence of device parameters on simulated photovoltaic performance.

One of my studies was accepted for the 12th IEEE International Conference on Sustainable Technology and Engineering (i-COSTE 2026). My finalized undergraduate thesis topic is Machine Learning-Based Prediction of Solar Cell Performance Using Offline Data; the thesis research has not yet started.

SCAPS-1D Modeling and Multi-Parameter Optimization of a CABTSe/MASnI₃ Double-Absorber Lead-Free Solar Cell

SCAPS-1D 12th IEEE International Conference on Sustainable Technology and Engineering (i-COSTE 2026) 1–3 November 2026 · Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh

OVERVIEW

The study uses SCAPS-1D to model a lead-free double-absorber thin-film solar cell under standard AM 1.5G illumination and examine how device parameters affect photovoltaic performance.

NUMERICAL MODELING METHODOLOGY

The accepted-paper device structure is a six-layer planar stack: FTO / ETL / CuAgBeSnSe₄ / MASnI₃ / Zn₃P₂ / Au. The ETL is shown in the conference-paper figure with the candidate materials WS₂, SnS₂, ZnSe, ZnO, PCBM and TiO₂. The figure also specifies the thickness of each layer and the Au contact value.

SIMULATION CONDITIONS RECORDED IN THE SUPPLIED FILE

  • Simulation platform: SCAPS-1D
  • Illumination: AM 1.5G, 1 Sun
  • Device architecture (substrate to top contact): FTO / ETL / CuAgBeSnSe₄ / MASnI₃ / Zn₃P₂ / Au
  • FTO thickness: 0.45 µm
  • ETL thickness: 0.05 µm; candidate materials shown: WS₂, SnS₂, ZnSe, ZnO, PCBM and TiO₂
  • CuAgBeSnSe₄ thickness: 0.20 µm
  • MASnI₃ thickness: 0.50 µm
  • Zn₃P₂ thickness: 0.10 µm
  • Au (gold) contact value shown: 5.1 eV

Other numerical input parameters and their literature sources were not included in the supplied project files.
